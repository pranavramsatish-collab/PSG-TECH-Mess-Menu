Privacy

PSG Tech Mess Menu · Last updated 20 September 2026
In one line

This app stores what you rate and what you report, along with your name, roll number and the email address of the Google account you sign in with, so the mess committee can act on it and so you can't rate the same meal twice.

Who runs this

The app is built and run by Pranav Ram Satish, a student at PSG College of Technology. It is not an official PSG College of Technology application and is not operated by the college or by the hostel administration. Contact: pranavram.sd@gmail.com.

What is collected

Nothing is collected until you rate a meal or report an issue. Browsing the menu collects nothing at all.

  • Your Google account — when you sign in, the app receives your email address, your name, and an account identifier from Google. It does not receive your password, your contacts, your files, or anything else in your Google account.
  • Your name and roll number — as you enter them.
  • Ratings — which mess and meal, on which date, a score out of five, and a comment if you write one.
  • Reports — which mess, what you wrote, and a photo if you attach one.

What is not collected

  • No location, ever.
  • No contacts, photos or files, apart from a photo you deliberately attach to a report.
  • No advertising or analytics trackers of any kind. There are no third-party scripts on the page beyond the web font.

Who can see it

  • The mess committee can see ratings and reports, including the name and roll number attached to them. That is the point: a report with nobody behind it can't be followed up.
  • Other students see only the average score for a meal. They cannot see who gave which score, and they cannot see your reports.
  • Nobody else. Nothing is sold, shared, or handed to any third party. The database refuses to return another student's reports even if the app asks for them.

Where it is stored

In a Supabase database (hosted PostgreSQL). Sign-in is handled by Supabase Auth using Google as the identity provider. Data is held for as long as the app runs; there is no fixed deletion schedule.

Your device also stores a few small settings locally — your theme choice, your name and roll number so you don't retype them, and which meals you have already rated. Those never leave your phone, and clearing your browser data removes them.

Getting your data removed

Email pranavram.sd@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and say what you want removed — a single rating, a single report, or everything. It will be deleted from the database. Removing a rating changes the average for that meal, which is expected.

You can also revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time from your Google account permissions page. That stops future sign-ins; it does not delete what is already stored, so email as well if that is what you want.

Two things worth saying plainly

Reports are not anonymous. Your name and roll number go with them. That is what lets the committee come back to you with an answer — and it is also a reason to think before you write something. If you want to raise something anonymously, this is not the right channel.

Signing in with Google proves you have a Google account, not that you live in the hostel. Anyone with a Google account can currently sign in.

Changes

If this changes, the date at the top changes with it. The app is small and run by one student; there is no mailing list to notify.
