About

This started as a question — what's for dinner? — that was annoyingly hard to answer without walking down to find out. It's a small, free tool for PSG Tech hostellers, built by a student. It isn't an official college service.

Serving G, AB and CD mess, across both the North and South Indian menus. If the menu is ever wrong or out of date, report it and it gets fixed.
