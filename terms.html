PSG Tech Mess Menu · Last updated 20 September 2026
In one line

A free student-built tool for checking the hostel mess menu and telling the mess committee what you think. Use it honestly; it comes with no guarantees.

What this is

A free web app that shows the PSG Tech hostel mess menu and lets hostel residents rate meals and report problems to the mess committee. It is built and run by Pranav Ram Satish, a student, as an independent project. It is not an official PSG College of Technology application, and neither the college nor the hostel administration operates it or is responsible for it.

Using it

You may use the app if you have a Google account. By rating a meal or filing a report you agree to these terms and to the privacy notice.

  • One rating per person per meal. Don't try to get round it by using another account or someone else's roll number.
  • Rate honestly. A rating is meant to describe the meal you actually ate.
  • Report real problems. Reports go to real people who have to read and act on each one. Don't use them for abuse, jokes, or anything about a named individual that you would not say to their face.
  • Photos you attach should be of the food or the mess. Don't upload photos of people without their agreement.
  • Don't attempt to break, overload, or extract data from the app or the database behind it.

Ratings or reports that break these rules may be deleted, and access may be withdrawn.

The menu may be wrong

The menu shown is a fixed timetable held in the app. The mess can and does change what it serves at short notice, and the app will not know. Treat it as a good guess, not a promise. Don't rely on it for anything that matters — an allergy, a fast, a medical requirement. Check with the mess.

No guarantees

The app is provided as-is, free, with no warranty of any kind. It may be unavailable, lose data, or stop working entirely. It depends on services (Supabase, Google, the host) that are outside anyone's control here. To the extent the law allows, the operator is not liable for any loss arising from using it or from relying on what it shows.

What you write

You keep whatever rights you have in your ratings, comments, reports and photos. By submitting them you allow the app and the mess committee to store them, show them to the committee, and include your scores in the average shown to other students. Your individual scores and your reports are not shown to other students — see the privacy notice.

Stopping

You can stop using the app at any time, revoke its access from your Google account permissions page, and email pranavram.sd@gmail.com to have your data removed.
